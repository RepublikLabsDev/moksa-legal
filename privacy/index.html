This policy explains what information the Moksa app collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Moksa mobile app for Android and iOS.

Who we are

Moksa is provided by [Republik Labs Limited, a company incorporated in Hong Kong, company number [number], registered address [address]] ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your information, contact us at [privacy@moksa domain].

The short version

  • Moksa is a self-custody wallet. Your recovery phrase, private keys and PIN are created and kept on your phone, encrypted. We never receive them, cannot see them and cannot recover them for you.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. You do not sign up to use Moksa.
  • To work, the app has to talk to blockchain networks and a few service providers. That means your public wallet addresses and the transactions you approve are sent to blockchain nodes, and your device's IP address is seen by every service the app connects to.
  • We use Google Firebase for app settings and usage statistics, and Sentry for crash reports. These are described below, including the device identifiers Firebase uses.
  • We do not sell your information.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your recovery phrase(s), private keys and PIN. These are encrypted on the device (XChaCha20-Poly1305) with a key held in your phone's secure hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore or Apple Keychain).
  • Your settings: language, display currency, light or dark mode, which networks are shown, whether balances are hidden, whether face or fingerprint unlock and "Lock when you leave" are on, and your choice about alerts.
  • Account names, colours and order that you set, and whether each wallet has been backed up.
  • Recent recipient addresses you have sent to, websites you saved in Explore, and a cache of your balances so the app opens quickly.
  • A count of wrong PIN attempts, used to slow down guessing.

If you delete the app or log out, this information is removed from the app. Without your recovery phrase, we cannot restore your wallet.

What leaves your phone, and why

1. Blockchain networks (needed for the wallet to work)

To show your balances and history and to send money, the app connects to public blockchain nodes and explorers for each network you use (for example Ethereum and compatible networks, Bitcoin, Solana and Zcash). These requests include:

  • your public wallet addresses (for Bitcoin, the app checks several addresses that belong to your wallet, which lets that service see they are linked);
  • the transactions you approve, already signed on your phone, so they can be broadcast;
  • your device's IP address, as with any internet connection.

These services are run by third parties, including [publicnode.com, llamarpc.com, monad.xyz, mempool.space, blockstream.info, emzy.de, solana.com, Blockchair, Blockscout, Etherscan and Jupiter (token names)]. We may change the providers we use. Information on a public blockchain, such as your addresses, balances and transactions, is public by design and cannot be deleted by us or anyone else.

2. Prices

The app fetches market prices from CoinGecko to show values in your chosen currency. Only the list of assets and the currency are sent, not your addresses. CoinGecko sees your IP address.

3. Network logos

Logos for each network are loaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), which sees your IP address and which logos are requested.

4. App configuration (Google Firebase Remote Config)

Each time it starts, the app fetches settings from Google Firebase Remote Config (for example, which networks and service endpoints to use, and whether an update is required). Firebase receives an app installation identifier, the app version, your platform, language and time zone, and your IP address.

5. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase. It automatically records events such as the app being opened, sessions, screens viewed and app updates, together with:

  • a Firebase installation identifier;
  • your device model, operating system and version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region, not precise location);
  • where available, your device's advertising identifier (on Android, the Google advertising ID; on iOS, Apple's advertising identifier only if your settings allow it) and, on Android, the app-specific Android ID.

We do not send your wallet addresses, balances or transactions to Analytics, and we do not use it to show you ads. [Confirm before publishing: see drafting note 1.] You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings.

6. Crash and error reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry, hosted in the European Union (Germany). It contains technical details such as the error, where in the app it happened, your device model and operating system, the app version and your IP address. For a sample of sessions, Sentry also receives performance timings.

We do not send screenshots. Before a report leaves your phone, the app removes anything that looks like a recovery phrase, private key or wallet address, and cuts web addresses down to the name of the service (for example, it keeps "mempool.space" but not the rest of the address).

7. App updates (Expo)

The app checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) at launch for improvements to the app. Expo receives your platform, the app's version and update channel, an installation identifier and your IP address.

8. Cloud backup (optional, iPhone only)

If you choose to back up your recovery phrase to iCloud, the app first encrypts it on your phone with a passphrase that only you know (Argon2id key derivation, then XChaCha20-Poly1305). Only the encrypted file goes to your own iCloud storage, together with a small unencrypted file holding the backup date, an optional hint you write, and a short fingerprint derived from your address so the app can match the backup to your wallet. We cannot read your backup, and if you forget the passphrase we cannot recover it. Apple's own privacy policy covers iCloud. [See drafting note 4.]

9. Websites and links you open

Explore lists third-party websites and services (for example decentralised exchanges and lending apps), and the app can open block-explorer pages. These open only when you tap them, in an in-app browser or another app, and are governed by those sites' own privacy policies. Moksa does not connect your wallet to them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the app does not send push notifications. The camera is used only to scan QR codes on your phone, and images are not stored or sent. Face or fingerprint checks are performed by your phone's operating system; we never receive biometric data.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: to scan QR codes for addresses.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: only if you turn on face or fingerprint unlock.
  • Internet access: to connect to the services described above.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate the wallet: show balances and history, prepare and broadcast the transactions you approve, and show prices;
  • keep the app working and secure: deliver updates, choose working service endpoints, and find and fix crashes and errors;
  • understand, in aggregate, how the app is used so we can improve it.

  • On your phone: until you log out, remove the wallet or delete the app.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): [90] days.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics): [14 months, or the retention set in our Analytics settings].

Security

Your wallet secrets are encrypted on your phone and protected by your PIN, and optionally by your face or fingerprint, with a limit on wrong PIN attempts. Crash reports are filtered to remove anything that looks like a recovery phrase, key or wallet address. No system is perfectly secure: keep your recovery phrase offline and private, and never share it with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from Moksa. We will never ask for your recovery phrase, private key or PIN.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use Moksa without giving us any contact details.
  • You can turn networks off in Settings so the app stops checking them.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or limit ad tracking, in your phone's settings.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your phone.
  • Depending on where you live (including under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, the EU and UK GDPR, and Australia's Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold your name or email, we may need you to give us an identifier (for example, the Firebase installation identifier shown [where]) to find your records. To make a request, contact [privacy@moksa domain].
